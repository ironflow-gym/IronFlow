# 2.4.4 — Food as a primary destination

Implemented locally on branch `2.4`. No commit, push, or deployment is authorised yet. The user requested a broader food review after approving the upload.

## Navigation

- Mobile bottom bar and desktop primary sidebar: Today, Workout, Food, Stats.
- Routine management remains under Today → Manage routines, which is always visible, including unfinished workouts and empty libraries. Routine management has Back to Today, and Today stays selected in primary navigation.
- Mobile navigation remains available during an unfinished workout, allowing food logging without finishing or abandoning the session.
- Food has direct pantry access, today's totals, logging, saved meals, target details, and food history. Existing target detail is collapsed so logging is encountered first.
- Food refreshes its local Today date on re-entry and stays mounted while hidden on other tabs, preserving food text drafts and local editing/history state during tab changes. Draft descriptions are held in memory, not persisted across reloads. Tab navigation remembers scroll positions for the current session.
- Stats → Nutrition is read-only: existing macro balance chart and recorded daily totals, with an Open food log shortcut. Logging callbacks and saved meal management were removed from Stats. Training/biometric review and their existing correction workflows are retained.
- Both desktop and mobile remember the selected Stats section when navigating away and back. Historical food entries can be edited from Food history, in addition to being repeated.
- Help text and release/cache metadata are updated. Storage keys and backup formats are unchanged.
