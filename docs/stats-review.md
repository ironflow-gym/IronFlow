# Stats review layout

Training, Body and Nutrition use the same navigation and section order on mobile
and desktop. Matching outlined header actions are provided for workout, measurement entry and Food.
The workout action resumes an unfinished workout first. Otherwise it previews
one planned workout for today, or offers a choice when several are planned.
With no planned workout it opens the empty Workout screen. Previewing or
cancelling does not start a session or complete an appointment. Block workout
links are retained when starting from Stats. Unavailable routines remain visible
with guidance to review the appointment in Today.
Section choices are device-local and do not change recorded data.

Training leads with recorded recent progress, a visible recovery summary, and an
estimated strength trend. Session history retains its detailed chart, exercise
selection, editing, export and calendar controls. Workload charts, consistency,
records and forecasts remain available in collapsible groups. AI review sends a
request only after Generate/Refresh is explicitly selected. Forecasts are estimates.

Body puts Add measurement and the dated latest reading/recorded averages first.
The weight trend uses recorded data and only shows averages with adequate readings.
Measurement history, derived indices, projections, detailed charts and labeled
photo assessment actions are available below. Adding a measurement is disabled
while a measurement draft is already open, so it cannot reset the draft's date.

Nutrition shows coverage for the last seven calendar days, averages across logged
days compared with current Food targets, and daily calories. Days with no entries
remain gaps, distinct from explicitly recorded zero intake. Partially logged days
are not asserted to be complete. The detailed radar, daily totals and targets are
available in collapsible groups; target changes remain in Food.
