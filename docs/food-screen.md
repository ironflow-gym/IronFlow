# Food screen

Logging stays at the top: manual entry, barcode, pantry and saved-meal shortcuts
are always visible. The AI description form expands in place and keeps its draft
when folded or when changing tabs. Estimates are reviewed before being logged.
The saved-meal shortcut uses the existing saved-meal dialogs, including creation
when there are no saved meals. Pantry and saved-meal management remain there.

Compact progress bars retain consumed/target values and accessible descriptions
of remaining or excess amounts. The calorie balance is also shown below them.
Targets & preferences is the one home for the existing target adjustment controls.
Review block nutrition remains beside the Food heading when available.

Today's food shows its count and last-added summary when closed. Expand it for
compact entries; Options contains Edit, Repeat and Delete, plus nutrition source
information. Deletion still requires confirmation. Food history starts closed and
retains the existing month/day browser, totals and historical editing actions.

AI, today's food, targets and history each remember their expanded state on this
device. These choices are separate from logged food, backups and cloud sync.
Preference-storage failure does not block logging. AI drafts retain their existing
in-memory behaviour; they are not saved by the section-preference mechanism.
